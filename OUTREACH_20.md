# sombr @ MSG (night 2, Tue 2026-11-24): BTS videographer outreach list

Compiled 2026-11-24. Openers tonight: Dove Cameron and Hannah Jadagu. Sources disagree on dates. Live Nation and Songkick list MSG shows on both Nov 23 and Nov 24, while Pollstar, Dork and Wikipedia call Nov 23 the finale.

**Confidence tags**
- **VERIFIED**: the contact appears in a source.
- **PATTERN GUESS**: built from the company's email format. The format and its source are given below the table.
- **HANDLE ONLY**: a social or LinkedIn handle was found, but no email.
- **NO DIRECT CHANNEL**: the person is confirmed, but no contact was found. Reach them through the person listed.

**Big caveats**
1. **Warner Records cut about a dozen staff in early Nov 2026.** Variety (Nov 5) named EVP Dalia Ganz, EVP Torie Mason and SVP Publicity Fount Lynch. Ganz ran the digital and marketing unit that Landig, Riddle and Morrow sat in. None of those three were reported cut, but the team may be in flux.
2. **Sacks & Co. may be his *former* publicist.** BookingAgentInfo lists Kate Rakvic and Carla Sacks as "used to be" publicists. It lists the current press team only by first name and initial: "Ceri R., Laura S., Lily G." A sombr artist-notes PDF does exist on sacksco.com (sacksco.com/notes/sombr_artist_notes.pdf), but I couldn't open it from this session.
3. **Promoter is mixed.** AEG Presents promoted Red Rocks and San Diego. Live Nation has event pages for both MSG dates and for Cleveland, Nashville, Columbus, Toronto and Philadelphia. No named promoter rep was found for MSG.
4. Wikipedia, CelebrityAccess and sacksco.com were blocked in this session, so I couldn't open them. Everything below comes from search-result snippets.
